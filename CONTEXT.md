# PyLanna Website

The public website of PyLanna, the Python user group community of Northern Thailand. It announces the community's events, shows the history of past events, and introduces the team.

## Language

**Event**:
A single gathering organised or co-organised by PyLanna, with one date, one venue and one registration link.
_Avoid_: Meetup (when you mean any gathering), session

**Meetup**:
A kind of Event: a short community gathering, usually a few hours.

**Devs Day**:
A kind of Event: a full-day conference with an agenda of Sessions. The first is CNX Devs Day 2026.

**CNX Devs Day 2026**:
The Devs Day held on Sat 12 Dec 2026, 09:30–16:30, at CMU STeP, Chiang Mai, with 50 Seats. "CNX" is the Chiang Mai airport code and stands for the city.
_Avoid_: CN Devs Day, CNX DevsDay, Devs Day 2026 (alone)

**Tagline**:
A slogan shown with an Event but not part of its name. For CNX Devs Day 2026 it is "NoAI === Not Only AI".

**Upcoming Event**:
The one Event whose date has not yet passed; it is the one the homepage promotes.

**Event History**:
The visible list of past Events, kept on the site rather than removed once an Event is over.
_Avoid_: Archive, old events

**Agenda**:
The ordered time slots of one Event, from registration to closing.

**Session**:
One presented topic slot in an Agenda, such as "Cloud Solution 101". Registration, meals and closing are breaks, not Sessions.
_Avoid_: Talk, track

**Speaker**:
A person who presents a Session. Shown only once confirmed; none are announced for CNX Devs Day 2026 yet.

**Partner**:
An organisation whose logo is shown on an Event as a supporter (Sukjai Lab, MongoDB, PDKM and I Gear Geek for CNX Devs Day 2026).
_Avoid_: Sponsor (unless the organisation is confirmed as paying)

**Seats**:
The fixed number of participants an Event can admit.

**Request to Join**:
The registration step on Luma where a person asks for a Seat and the Host must approve it. Approval is not automatic, so a request is not a confirmed Seat.
_Avoid_: Register NOW, RSVP, ticket

**Host**:
The person who owns the Event's registration page and approves requests. For CNX Devs Day 2026, Piti Champeethong.

**Presenter**:
The community shown as the organiser of an Event. Always PyLanna; a co-organiser such as THMUG is named alongside it.

## Relationships

- An **Upcoming Event** moves into **Event History** once its date has passed.
- An **Event** has one **Agenda**, made of **Sessions**, and zero or more **Speakers** and **Partners**.
- The **Host** approves each **Request to Join**, up to the number of **Seats**.
